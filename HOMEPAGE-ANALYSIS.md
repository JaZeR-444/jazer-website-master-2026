## 🎯 Key Issues & Opportunities

### **🔴 Critical Issues**

#### 1. **Too Many "TBA" / "Coming Soon" Messages**
**Problem:** 
- "New Era: Coming in 2026" (hero)
- "Coming in 2026" (announcement bar)
- "Next Release (TBA)" (featured)
- "TBA" (press section)
- "TBA" x3 (What's Next section)
- **7 instances of "not ready yet" messaging**

**Impact:** Feels incomplete, unconfident, not ready for visitors

**Solution:** Replace placeholder content with:
- Real value propositions
- Existing content (if any)
- Clear "launching soon" date if known
- Remove empty sections entirely

---

#### 2. **Weak Value Proposition**
**Current Hero Copy:**
> "Cinematic music and visuals for a new era."

**Problems:**
- Too vague ("new era" is meaningless)
- Doesn't answer: "Why should I care?"
- No unique selling point (USP)
- Generic buzzwords (cinematic, new era)

**Better Alternatives:**
```
Option 1 (Genre-specific):
"Electronic beats meet cinematic storytelling."

Option 2 (Emotion-focused):
"Music that moves you. Visuals that stay with you."

Option 3 (Direct):
"Where sound becomes an experience."

Option 4 (Story-driven):
"Sonic journeys for the dreamers and doers."
```

---

#### 3. **No Social Proof**
**Missing:**
- Streaming stats (if available)
- Collaborations
- Press mentions (even small ones)
- Fan testimonials
- Social media follower counts
- Playlist features

**Add Instead:**
- "As heard on [Spotify playlist]"
- "Featured on [blog/radio]"
- "10K+ streams on [platform]"
- "Working with [artist/producer]"
- Real quotes from fans/industry

---

#### 4. **Unclear Target Audience**
**Who is this for?**
- Music fans? (which genre?)
- Visual art enthusiasts?
- Content creators?
- Industry professionals?

**Current messaging is too broad** - appeals to no one specifically

**Fix:** Choose 1-2 primary audiences and speak directly to them

---

### **🟡 Major Opportunities**

#### 5. **Weak Call-to-Action Hierarchy**
**Current CTAs:**
- "Listen Now" (hero) → goes to empty music page
- "Explore" (hero) → scrolls down
- "Visit Music" + "Videos & Visuals" (featured)
- "Get updates" (email capture)

**Issues:**
- Primary CTA goes to empty page (bad UX)
- Too many competing CTAs (decision paralysis)
- No clear "next step" for visitors

**Better Hierarchy:**
```
Primary CTA: Follow on [Spotify/Instagram/YouTube]
  → Immediate value, builds audience

Secondary CTA: Join waitlist / Get updates
  → Captures leads for launch

Tertiary: Explore (internal navigation)
  → For curious visitors
```

---

#### 6. **Missing Personality**
**Current tone:** Professional but bland
- Reads like a template
- No voice, humor, or edge
- Doesn't reflect cyberpunk aesthetic in copy

**Add Personality Through:**
- Unique section titles ("Signal Check" vs "Featured")
- Brand voice in descriptions
- Easter eggs or hidden details
- Behind-the-scenes glimpses
- Personal story elements

---

#### 7. **Brand Banner Section is Weak**
**Current:**
> "A focused beam of sound and visuals."

**Problem:** Generic mission statement, no differentiation

**Stronger Alternatives:**
```
"Not just music. A frequency."
"Sound designed for your midnight drives and early mornings."
"Where every track tells a story, every visual has meaning."
"Built for headphone listeners and screen watchers."
```

---

#### 8. **Press/Features Section is a Red Flag**
**Currently:** Empty card saying "TBA"

**Options:**
1. **Remove it entirely** (best if you have nothing)
2. **Replace with "Journey So Far"** timeline
3. **Replace with "Influences"** section
4. **Replace with "Latest Update"** (blog-style)

**Never show an empty "Press" section** - looks unprofessional

---

#### 9. **"What's Next" Section Adds No Value**
**Current:** 3 cards all saying "TBA"

```
  - Collabs in progress
```

#### 10. **Pathways Grid is Redundant**
